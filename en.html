01 / COMING HOME

The same country.
A fresh perspective.

My name is Emmanuel. I left Benin to study in France. Two years later, I returned for a three-month computer science internship in Allada.

A train from Angers to Paris, then a six-hour flight. We land at 11 p.m. My family does not even know I am here yet. The joy of being back and a certain nostalgia mingle together.

A local SIM card, a contact who comes to pick us up, a first night in an unfurnished apartment. The next day, we head to Allada. Coming home also means navigating these small challenges and finding your place again.

02 / SHARING KNOWLEDGE

Writing code.
Building connections.

In Allada, computer science feels most meaningful when it helps others.

01

Rebuilding the CIDEV website

Replacing an old WordPress website that was slow and difficult to maintain with a clearer, modern experience that works on phones and computers alike.

02

Sharing essential digital skills

Helping run computer workshops for young people in Allada. Explaining, listening, adapting: teaching goes far beyond technology.

03

Learning from the unexpected

A smart irrigation system was meant to respond to soil moisture. Without funding, it never got off the ground. A disappointment, but also a lesson in working in the field.

04 / STEPPING BACK

A country full of potential.
Real challenges, too.

01 — TOURISM

Growing without leaving people behind.

Tourism creates opportunities for hotels, restaurants and transport. But rising prices can also put pressure on the families who live here all year round.

02 — DIGITAL ACCESS

Connecting ideas.

During my internship, I saw how a slow or expensive connection can hold a project back. Internet access remains essential for education and young people’s initiatives.

03 — YOUNG PEOPLE

Making room for talent.

A degree does not always lead to a suitable job. Bringing universities, businesses and young entrepreneurs closer together could open new paths.

“Computer science is not only about technology. It can also be a tool for learning and finding your place.”EMMANUEL · AFTER RETURNING FROM ALLADA
